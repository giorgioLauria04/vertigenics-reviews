Safety Considerations and Potential Side Effects

Vertigenics is generally well-tolerated. However, individuals taking blood pressure medications, antihypertensives, or tricyclic antidepressants should consult their doctor first, as some botanical ingredients may interact with these medications.

Vertigix, a similarly named prescription medication, can cause drowsiness and dry mouth. While Vertigenics is a different product, users should be aware of potential sedative effects from ingredients like Valerian Root and Passionflower if included in certain formulations.
